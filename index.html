Research result · MSc thesis

Equal on day one.
1.9× the oil.

I pooled all 109 hydraulic fracturing jobs across two carbonate oilfields and split them by the fracture they produced: a simple two-wing fracture, or a complex, multi-branched network. The first-day gain is almost the same. The difference is how long it lasts.
